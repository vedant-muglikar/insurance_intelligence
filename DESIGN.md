# Design System: PolicyLens

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Forest Floor"**

A dark green-black ground with one warm amber accent, the way a lamp reads in a wood at dusk. The system is calm, dense with real numbers, and quiet everywhere except the one place the eye should go. Dark is the default theme; a light "stone" theme is equally first-class and swaps every semantic token, not a filter. The theme is saved as `pl-theme` in localStorage and applied by an inline script in `app/layout.tsx` before first paint.

The landing and login are built from the product's own output: the hero proof is a real estimate-engine result in `components/landing/PolicyPreview.tsx`, not a drawn imitation. Layout is left-aligned and asymmetric, motion is CSS-first, and decoration is withheld. The system replaced an earlier ledger-paper identity; remnants of it survive only inside the tool (see Carried Items).

**Key Characteristics:**
- One accent (amber) across the whole page, locked in both themes.
- Hairline borders and one tinted shadow; no layered glow.
- Geist for words, Geist Mono for every figure.
- Tokens are semantic CSS custom properties in `app/theme.css`; Tailwind palette scales are remapped to them so the tool follows the theme.
- Page theme lock: sections never invert against the page theme.

## Colors

A green-black forest ground, mint-grey text, one amber accent, and four quiet status hues. Values below are the dark theme; the light theme values are in the sidecar `extensions.themes` and in the Light Theme list.

### Primary
- **Lamp Amber** (#dca94b dark, #92610a light): the single accent. Primary buttons, the hero accent line, active tab and focus states. In light it darkens to Amber Ink to keep text contrast. Hover is Amber Hi (#ebc374 dark, #a8730f light). Text on it is #1a1204 dark, #fff8e8 light.

### Neutral
- **Forest Ground** (#0c1411): page background (`--bg`). Light: Stone Ground (#eef1ee).
- **Forest Surface** (#111b17), **Forest Card** (#15211c), **Card Raised** (#1a2923), **Sunk** (#09100e), **Raised** (#203029): the surface ladder (`--surface`, `--card`, `--card2`, `--sunk`, `--raised`). Light: #f6f8f5, #fbfcfa, #f1f4f0, #e4e9e4, #ffffff.
- **Forest Border** (#243630), strong (#2f453d), strongest (#46645a): hairlines (`--border`, `--border2`, `--border3`). Light: #d3dbd4, #bcc8bf, #97a89d.
- **Mist Text** (#e8efea), muted (#b3c2b9), subtle (#93a69b), faint (#86998e): text ladder. Light: #14201b, #3f5249, #4f6258, #566a60.

### Status
- **Ok Green** (#5fc59a; ink #86d6b0; light #1d7a55), **Warn** (the amber, #dca94b; light #92610a), **Deny Coral** (#ec7a68; ink #f5a092; light #b83a2e), **Info Blue** (#7da2de; ink #9dbbea; light #2f5aa8). A plum (#a99be0; light #6b3fa0) exists as `--plum` for the tool.

### Light Theme (stone)
Ground #eef1ee, surface #f6f8f5, card #fbfcfa, text #14201b, brand #92610a, brand-hi #a8730f, on-brand #fff8e8, shadow `0 14px 30px -20px rgba(20,50,38,0.32)`.

### Named Rules
**The One Accent Rule.** Amber is the only chromatic accent on landing, login and chrome. Status hues carry meaning (covered, caution, denied, note) and are never used as decoration.
**The Theme Lock Rule.** The whole page follows the active theme. No section is inverted to the opposite theme for contrast; the CTA band is in-theme.
**The Remap Rule.** Tailwind slate, gray, zinc, neutral, stone map to the forest ladder; emerald, green, teal, lime to ok green; amber, yellow, orange to amber; red, rose, pink, fuchsia to coral; blue, cyan, sky, indigo, violet, purple to info blue. The light scales are inverted so legacy utilities keep their meaning. Do not hard-code Tailwind hex for new work; use the semantic variables.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Geist (with system-ui, sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Geist
**Label/Mono Font:** Geist Mono (with ui-monospace, Menlo, Consolas)

**Character:** One sans family doing display and text, weights 400 to 900, headings at 650 with tight negative tracking. Mono carries the numbers so they align and read as data. Both load via `next/font` as `--font-geist-sans` and `--font-geist-mono`, mapped to `--font-sans`, `--font-display`, `--font-mono`.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (650, clamp(2.3rem, 4.2vw, 3.5rem), 1.06, -0.035em): landing H1 only, with one accent line in amber.
- **Headline** (650, clamp(2.2rem, 3.8vw, 3rem)): landing section headings.
- **Title** (650, clamp(1.7rem, 3.2vw, 2.5rem)): CTA band and large panel headings.
- **Body** (400, clamp(1rem, 1.2vw, 1.125rem), 1.65): lede capped at 54ch; other prose 44 to 62ch.
- **Label** (600, 15px, -0.005em): buttons; small buttons 13.5px.
- **Figure** (Geist Mono, 500, tabular numerals): every rupee amount, percentage and count.

### Named Rules
**The Mono Figures Rule.** Every figure is Geist Mono with tabular numerals, in prose rows, tables, gauges and counters.

## Layout

Content sits in a 1200px max container, left-aligned. The hero is asymmetric: copy on one side, the real-engine proof panel on the other, with a fluid gap (`clamp(36px, 6vw, 88px)`). Steps are a vertical drawn sequence, features a 2-column grid, with a cost-model card holding a range bar, an in-theme CTA band and a footer. Narrow content blocks use 980px. Breakpoints in use on landing: 1023px (wide layouts collapse), 899px (nav to compact), 767px (single column). The login page pairs a proof panel with a form that switches between sign in and create account, with a password strength meter.

## Elevation & Depth

Mostly flat: depth comes from the surface ladder and hairline borders. A single tinted shadow token `--shadow` lifts panels (dark `0 18px 40px -22px rgba(0,0,0,0.65)`, light `0 14px 30px -20px rgba(20,50,38,0.32)`). Primary buttons also carry a tinted amber drop with a 2px inset ring (see Carried Items).

### Named Rules
**The One Shadow Rule.** One tinted `--shadow` per theme. No stacked glows, no hard offset shadows.

## Shapes

Soft but disciplined: containers 14px, controls (buttons, inputs, option pills) 10px, result-tab tops 8px, bars 2px, ticks 3px, marks 4 to 6px, the delta pill fully round (999px), the preview body `0 14px 14px 14px`. Borders are 1px hairlines; buttons use a 2px border that is transparent or the fill colour.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** 10px radius, 13px 22px padding (small: 8px 15px), 2px border.
- **Primary** (`.pl-btn-primary`): amber fill, dark ink text, hover to Amber Hi with 1px lift, active 1px press and 0.985 scale. Trailing arrow icon nudges 3px right on hover.
- **Ghost** (`.pl-btn-ghost`): transparent, text colour, `--border3` outline; hover shifts the border to amber and tints the fill 8%.
- **Disabled:** 50% opacity.

### Cards / Containers
14px radius, `--card` fill, `--border` hairline, `--shadow` only for lifted panels.

### Proof Panel
`components/landing/PolicyPreview.tsx` renders real estimate-engine output: tabs, definition-list rows and totals, with figures in mono. It is the hero proof and the login proof.

### Navigation and Chrome
Brand mark and wordmark, theme toggle (Phosphor icons, short colour-only crossfade via `html.pl-theme-fade`), nav active state driven by IntersectionObserver. Tool chrome in `app/ledger.css` provides result tabs and status badges.

### Motion
CSS-only scroll behaviour behind `@supports`: `animation-timeline: scroll()` for the progress bar, `view()` for reveals and the step-line draw, with a non-supporting fallback. Staggered load-in via `animation-delay` (0.7s, 0.09s steps). Stat counters use `animate()` from motion/react writing to DOM nodes. A global `prefers-reduced-motion` override lives in `app/ledger.css`; `theme.css` also disables smooth scroll under reduced motion.

### Icons
Phosphor (`@phosphor-icons/react`) on landing, login, Brand and ThemeToggle.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** use the semantic variables (`--bg`, `--card`, `--text`, `--brand`, `--ok`, `--warn`, `--deny`, `--info`) so both themes work.
- **Do** set every figure in Geist Mono with tabular numerals.
- **Do** show real engine output as product proof.
- **Do** use `animation-timeline` guarded by `@supports`, and IntersectionObserver for nav state.
- **Do** keep landing and login copy free of em-dashes.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add a second accent or use status hues decoratively.
- **Don't** invert a section against the page theme.
- **Don't** attach window scroll listeners.
- **Don't** build fake product UI out of divs where real output can be rendered.
- **Don't** add eyebrows, section numerals, decorative dots, marquees or scroll cues.

## Carried Items (not canonized)

These exist in the build but are not rules for new surfaces:
- The tool interior (`/app`, `components/policy/*`) keeps ledger-era chrome: the statement block with margin rules (`app/ledger-tool.css`), stamp-style badges, the rotated `.pl-stamp`, and Lucide icons. Do not extend these to new screens.
- The primary button's 2px inset "stamp" ring in `app/ledger.css` is a ledger-era remnant.
- Some em and en dashes remain in tool strings.
- `app/theme.css` is generated by a script outside the repo; regenerating it needs the token table (not in the repo).
- The landing hero lede is 28 words.
- The type ramp is wide (about 20 distinct sizes from 10.5px to 52px plus fluid clamps) and the radius scale has 12 steps; both are documented as shipped and could be consolidated later.
- `app/globals.css` still has uppercase letter-spaced labels (`.14em`, `.1em`) and 800-weight display sizes from earlier work; unverified for which surfaces use them.
