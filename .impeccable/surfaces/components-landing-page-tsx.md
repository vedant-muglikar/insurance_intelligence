# Surface brief: PolicyLens public pages and tool (Bahi-Khata world)

Scope and mode: landing (Persuade), login, and the /app tool (Operate) share one world. Dark default, light toggle.
Audience, job, action: patients and families planning an admission; learn their patient share, then launch the preflight.
Proof and content: illustrative sample ledgers (labelled), real cost-model example (Total Knee Replacement, Mumbai, about ₹3.44L to ₹4.61L, typical ₹4.04L). No invented customers or accuracy claims.
Constraints: name is PolicyLens (renamed from ClaimLens by the user); keep all features and copy meaning; warm not clinical; restrained motion that never slows tasks.
Unresolved: none.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Your policy is read like the family ledger: every clause a ruled line, every deduction totalled at the foot. Refuses the split-hero-plus-card-grid insurtech page.

OWN-WORLD: Red-cloth account book. Dark mode is the cover at night (oxblood-black ground, cream ink, gold-leaf rules and brand actions); light mode is the page (warm paper, cloth-red headings and actions). Ruled lines, a rupee column, double-rule totals, round ink stamps for actions and statuses. Credit in verdigris, debits in vermilion, ink-blue for notes. Bitter slab for headings, Source Sans 3 for text, Spline Sans Mono for figures.

STORY: A visitor sees a ledger that totals their bill line by line, believes the arithmetic is auditable, and launches the preflight.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width ledger spread. Left page: PolicyLens wordmark, headline "Policy-to-Patient. Coverage Preflight." set large, the lede, and the Launch Preflight stamp button. Right page: a live ruled ledger totalling a sample bill with a rupee column, a running total, and ink-stamped deductions appearing line by line. Nav is a ruled header with section tabs, sign in, and the theme toggle.

FORM: Pick card (impeccable's pick, seed key 09669d3d), ledger spread with margin ruling.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
